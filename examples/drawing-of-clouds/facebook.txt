There’s something about a bright summer sky – those fluffy clouds drifting by, catching the sunlight. I was trying to capture that feeling, that sense of movement and light, really trying to show how quickly it shifts. Layering wiggly lines and shapes, building up the image with a little bit of movement, trying to not draw a single shape, but to draw many shapes, to represent the dynamic nature of it all. It’s very difficult to do in a drawing, to hold onto that fleeting moment. 

I wanted to create something that felt serene, contemplative – a little reminder to slow down and really notice the beauty around us. To remember this moment and you want to remember as many moments like this as possible. What does a cloud make you think of?
