There's something about watching clouds drift across a summer sky – a constant, gentle movement of
light and fluffy shapes. It’s very difficult to capture that feeling in a drawing, to move beyond a
single, fixed form. I’ve layered wiggly lines, many shapes, mimicking the way the clouds shift and
change with every passing moment, a reminder that this moment in time is transient. It’s a quiet,
joyful contemplation. What simple beauty do you find today?
