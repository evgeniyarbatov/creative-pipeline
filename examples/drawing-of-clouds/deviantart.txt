It's incredibly difficult to truly capture something like this – the way light dances across a
summer sky, the sheer, shifting movement of clouds. I spent a long time just… watching. Really
watching. The fluffy shapes, the way they seem to billow and morph with every passing breeze. My
intention wasn’t to draw a single, defined cloud, but to build it up, layer upon layer, with wiggly
lines and countless smaller shapes. I wanted to convey that dynamic quality, that sense of constant
change, of light playing tricks on your eye. The goal was to try and hold onto that feeling, that
moment of serene contemplation—to remember this, and so many moments like it—as a quiet reminder of
impermanence, of the beauty in simply being present. There’s something about observing clouds that
encourages you to ‘think bigger,’ to ‘dream bigger,’ doesn’t it? I hope this piece brings a little
of that wonder to you.
