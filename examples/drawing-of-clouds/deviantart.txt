There’s something profoundly moving about a summer sky, isn’t there? Spending time just watching
clouds drift across it, the way the light shifts and paints them with fleeting colours – it’s very
difficult to capture that feeling in a drawing, but that’s always the challenge, isn’t it? I wanted
to avoid rendering a single, definitive shape for each cloud, instead creating a multitude of
shifting forms, wiggly lines that suggest constant movement and transformation. It's about not
drawing a single shape, but to draw many shapes, echoing the dynamic nature of the sky and the
transient beauty of a moment. Layering these lines, creating a sense of depth and flow, hopefully
bring you a sense of joy of looking at the clouds. This moment in time, it’s passing, it’s passing,
it’s never to return. I hope this piece encourages a bit of quiet contemplation, a chance to pause
and see the world with a little more wonder – to think bigger, to dream bigger, simply by observing
and reflecting on the beauty that’s already surrounding us.
