It’s often hard to truly represent something like this – the movement of clouds, the way light shifts across the sky. I spent time observing carefully, focusing on the shapes and how they change with the breeze. I wasn’t aiming for a specific cloud image, but built it up with layered lines and smaller forms to capture that constant movement and the play of light. My intention was to hold onto a sense of quiet contemplation – a reminder of change and beauty in the present moment. It’s interesting how observing clouds can encourage you to consider bigger ideas. I hope this piece offers a little of that feeling.
