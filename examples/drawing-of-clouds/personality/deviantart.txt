The summer sky has a particular stillness that’s worth observing. It's a challenge to represent that
in a drawing – to capture the way clouds shift and change color as the light moves across them. I’ve
focused on creating many individual lines, avoiding solid shapes, to convey the constant movement
and transformation I see. Layering these lines helps build depth and suggests a sense of flow,
hopefully creating a quiet moment of reflection when you look at them. These moments are fleeting,
and I hope this piece inspires a pause to observe the world around us, to consider it with a bit
more attention and perhaps, to think more expansively.
