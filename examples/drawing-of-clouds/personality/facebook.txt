A bright summer sky, with drifting clouds catching the sunlight, is a complex subject to represent. I was working to capture the feeling of movement and shifting light, focusing on how quickly it changes. I built up the image with wiggly lines and shapes, avoiding a single, defined form to represent its dynamic nature. It’s challenging to hold onto that fleeting moment in a drawing. I wanted to create something serene and contemplative – a reminder to slow down and observe the beauty around us. What do clouds make you think of?
