So this one's the clouds piece. I don't know, I was just lying on the grass watching them and I needed to draw it, like literally needed to, I don't know why exactly.

I wanted them to feel like they're moving, like drifting, but also — and this is going to sound weird — also frozen? Like caught in a single moment. I kept doing these wiggly overlapping lines because clouds never just sit still, they're always kind of reshaping themselves while you're looking at them.

It's about joy, honestly, just watching clouds is one of the most joyful dumb things you can do. ... actually no, it's kind of sad too. I don't know. Maybe both. There's something sad about how a shape you like is already gone by the time you've registered it.

I really, really wanted the colors to feel like a summer evening, not blue exactly, more golden-grey, I'm not totally sure I got there.

Oh — this reminds me of being on the roof with my brother, we used to climb up and just lie there and point at shapes for hours. I don't know if that's actually related to this piece or if I'm just remembering it because I was lying down again. Probably nothing. Or maybe it's everything, I don't know.

Not sure if the bottom of the piece needs to be darker, it feels a little flat compared to the top right now.
