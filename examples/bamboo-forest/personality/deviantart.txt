There’s a quiet in a forest that consistently draws me in. I’ve been working to translate that
feeling from a run I took in Sapam, Vietnam – a small, tightly packed bamboo forest – onto paper. It
wasn’t a large space, perhaps twenty or thirty trees, but it felt intensely significant. I
repeatedly found myself returning there, often in the early mornings, the ground soft underfoot.

It became a space for intentional pause. A deliberate slowing down, a moment for genuine reflection.
You feel completely encompassed by that landscape, which is exactly what I was trying to capture.
The bamboo itself invites contemplation. This drawing came about two weeks after the run; it’s a
reminder to seek out those moments where the world simply slows down.
