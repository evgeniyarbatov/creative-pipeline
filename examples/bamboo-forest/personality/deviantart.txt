The stillness in places like Sapam, Vietnam, is remarkable. I was running a 100-kilometer race, and
a large part of it involved running through dense bamboo forests. The trees were packed closely
together – often in clusters of thirty or more – creating a dark, cool passage. The shifting shadows
and the light filtering through the stalks induced a disorienting effect, as if the landscape were a
continuous, deliberate incline.

It wasn’t about finishing the distance; it was about observing. These forests prompted a necessary
pause, offering a moment for reflection and a chance to simply *be* within the quiet. It’s uncommon
to find such a shift in perspective during a run. I wanted to hold onto that feeling – the sense of
slowing down, and appreciating the natural beauty around me. These forests provide a chance to
pause, to think. It's a moment I want to cherish.
