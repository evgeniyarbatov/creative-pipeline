- Clusters of 20-30 trees packed close, rising like a silent hill.
- Steep hill near the end of a 100-kilometer run.
- Forced a slower pace; a necessary pause within the burn.
- Disorienting sense that the whole world tilted to that hill.
- Quiet appreciation and stillness, a deep breath in the forest.
