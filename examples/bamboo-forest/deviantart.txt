There’s something about the quiet of a forest that just… settles in you. I’ve been trying to capture the feeling of that particular encounter on paper – a small bamboo forest I stumbled upon during a run in Sapam, Vietnam. It wasn’t grand, maybe twenty or thirty trees tightly clustered, but it felt utterly profound. I kept finding myself drawn to it, often in the darkness of the early mornings, the ground soft beneath my feet. 

It became a space for pause, really. A deliberate slowing down, a moment of genuine reflection. You feel like the whole earth consists of that hill, the way it dominates the landscape. It’s like one of those moments you want to hold onto, to appreciate the stillness. The bamboo itself – they give you pause, they get you to think. This drawing came about roughly two weeks after the run, an attempt to bottle that feeling of serenity, that intense quiet contemplation. It's a reminder to find those spaces, those moments, where the world just… slows down.
