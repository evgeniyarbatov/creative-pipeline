There’s a particular bamboo forest I stumbled upon during a run in Sapam, Vietnam. Twenty or thirty trees, clustered together, often appearing dark as I passed through them early in the morning. It was a quiet space, a chance to really slow down, to walk deliberately among the soft, leafy ground. That moment—the way it demanded a pause—felt like a vital part of the whole journey, especially during that challenging climb.  It’s lovely to capture these quiet encounters.
