Spending a few weeks back in this drawing, remembering a run through the bamboo forests of Sapam,
Vietnam. The sheer density of those trees – groups of twenty, thirty – creating this incredible
stillness, this feeling of being completely enveloped. It was like the whole hill just… existed, a
deliberate, quiet pace. A necessary pause amidst the 100 kilometers. What moments like these remind
you to simply *be*?
